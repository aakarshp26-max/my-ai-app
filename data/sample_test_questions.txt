MUN Command Center — testing checklist

Use these only as example questions. The correct answer depends on the documents you upload.

1. What is the procedure for raising a point of personal privilege?
2. What is the deadline for submitting a draft resolution?
3. What are the attendance requirements?
4. Which committee is assigned to a particular agenda?
5. What is the venue of the closing ceremony?

For question 5, choose a fact that is not in your uploaded documents. The assistant should say it cannot find the information rather than guessing.
